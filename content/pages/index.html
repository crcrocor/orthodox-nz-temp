New Zealand Deanery of the Russian Orthodox Church Outside of Russia

Welcome to the website of the New Zealand Deanery of the Russian Orthodox Church Outside of Russia (ROCOR). The deanery belongs to the Australian and New Zealand Diocese. Our ruling bishop is Archbishop George (Schaefer); our dean is Archpriest Vladimir Boikov, rector of the Church of the Resurrection of Christ in Auckland.

Here you will find our news and newsletters, the service schedule of the Auckland parish, our contact details and an introduction to the Orthodox faith. We will be glad to see you at the Divine Services.
